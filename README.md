# Walmart Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Walmart sales data to identify important business trends and insights using Python and Pandas.

The analysis covers sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer contribution, category performance, state-wise sales, and monthly sales trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing products
* Analyze sales by category and state
* Study monthly sales trends
* Identify the most profitable products
* Analyze customer sales contribution
* Calculate profit margins
* Generate meaningful business insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Google Colab
* Microsoft Excel

## 📈 Key Insights

* Sales performance varies across products, categories, and states.
* A small number of products contribute significantly to total sales.
* Sales and profit should be analyzed together because high sales do not always result in high profitability.
* Monthly analysis helps identify high- and low-performing periods.
* Top customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Profit margin provides an additional perspective when evaluating product performance.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing products and categories.
* Monitor products with low profit margins.
* Analyze high-performing states to understand demand drivers.
* Use monthly sales trends for better sales and inventory planning.
* Develop strategies to retain high-value customers.
* Use data-driven insights to improve overall profitability.
